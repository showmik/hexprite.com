Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Hexprite. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy will inform you as to how we look after your personal data when you visit our website or use our application.

2. Data We Collect

The Hexprite desktop application operates entirely offline and locally on your machine. We do not collect, transmit, or store any telemetry data, usage metrics, or user-generated content from within the application.

If you choose to donate or purchase a premium version, payment processing is handled securely by third-party providers. We do not store your credit card details.

3. Cookies

Our website uses minimal cookies necessary for core functionality (such as saving your dark mode preference). We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our official GitHub repository.
